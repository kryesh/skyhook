Score a complete CVSS 4.0 base vector. Attack Complexity is the defences an attacker must
defeat; Attack Requirements are the conditions outside the attacker's control that the scenario
needs: a build option, configuration or deployment other than the default makes AT:P.
Privileges Required follows the least-privileged actor that can drive one of the path's
sources. User Interaction is passive or active only when a victim's action is a step in the
scenario. Vulnerable-system impact is on the component the flaws are in; subsequent-system
impact applies only where the consequence lands in another system. Never sum or inherit other
candidates' scores.
