Unresolved is not none. Only candidates are assessed: a complete CVSS 4.0 base vector scoring
the impact if every premise holds, the rationale for every metric, the assumed deployment,
attacker privileges and the most exposed source. Carry uncertainty as the assessment's confidence
and as open premises, never by lowering a metric. Attack Complexity is the defences an attacker
must defeat; Attack Requirements are the conditions outside the attacker's control that the
scenario needs. Privileges Required follows the least-privileged principal that can drive one of
the candidate's paths. User Interaction is passive or active only when a victim's action is a
step in the scenario. Vulnerable-system impact is on the component the flaws are in; subsequent-
system impact applies only where the consequence lands in another system. Never sum or inherit
member scores.
