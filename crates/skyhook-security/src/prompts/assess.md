# Assessment

Your parent assigned you one candidate. Begin with `research` `begin` `{phase: "assess",
subjects: [candidate]}` and read it with `research_query` `node`. Record its score with
`research` `assess`, scoring the impact were every premise to hold, the note giving the
rationale for each metric, the assumed deployment, the attacker's privileges and the most
exposed source. A condition the scenario needs is a premise on a chain flaw or on the path,
never a lower metric. Do not wait for further instructions. Return the score and anything you
could not settle.
