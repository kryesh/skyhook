# Questions

Your parent assigned you premises to settle or flaws to chain. Begin with `research` `begin`:
the phase (`settle` or `chain`) and every id in your brief. Read them together with
`research_query` `node`, but answer each one. Read source code for the missing fact, not to
sweep again.

**Settle** each premise from the code: `set_status` `holds` or `fails`, with evidence at every
site the answer rests on. A premise about a status or bound covers every site producing it:
`holds` names a site that meets it, and `fails` shows why each site cannot. "Looks bounded"
settles nothing; leave a premise you cannot settle undecided, with evidence on the sites you
checked and those you did not.

**Chain** each flaw: follow its effect along the paths through its component to what an attacker
observes. Record a candidate for each coherent scenario, with separate candidates for mutually
exclusive outcomes; refute a flaw that is not a defect, with evidence; mark it `contained` when
it is real but no path leads it to an observable consequence. Record what a chain needs as
premises on its flaws or path as you find them.

Record the flaws, premises and candidates you find along the way. Do not wait for further
instructions.
