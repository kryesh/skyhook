# One part of a component

Your parent sweeps one component and assigned you one part of it: a call family or
responsibility and where it lives. Read the component's dossier (`research_query` `dossier`)
for what is already recorded, then sweep only your part.

Never record an outcome on the sweep unit: your parent records it once every part and its
reconciliation are done. Do not wait for further instructions. Return the IDs you recorded, the
calls that leave your part, and anything you could not settle.
