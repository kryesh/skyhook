# Path review

Your parent assigned you a path-review unit or a candidate to assess.

**A path-review unit.** `research_query` `unit` names its path, and `path_work` lists what the
path still owes: flaws without its standing decision and `contributes` decisions no live
candidate of the path builds on. Settle all of it; candidates are assessed once the review ends.

Read the path (`path`) and each reached component's `dossier`; build on what the sweep recorded
instead of re-reading the component whole.
Review what is particular to this path: what its sources control, the state and configuration
they set, the call order, the transitions between components, and the error and alternate
paths. For each listed flaw record one exposure with `research` `add_exposure`: unreachable, no
consequence, or contributes, with its conditions and a short ordered route of locations marked
inspected, inferred or assumed.
